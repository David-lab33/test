dog

Loyal, affectionate, and remarkably diverse, the dog (Canis lupus familiaris) has rightfully earned its title as "man's best friend." As the first domesticated animal, dogs have shared an evolutionary journey with humans for thousands of years, transitioning from wild predators to cherished family members and invaluable working partners. Their defining characteristic is an extraordinary ability to read human emotion and communicate through body language, vocalizations, and unyielding devotion.
